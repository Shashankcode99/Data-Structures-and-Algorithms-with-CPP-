2 pointer technique.

Definition:
2 pointer technique is basically reducing the effort of doing one job/task by simply dividing the effort into two person or 2 pointer. 

Example:
Let's say, a delivery guy has to deliver a parcel on 4th floor. 
So if we will take the brute force, the delivery guy will go to the 4th floor and then will come back to the ground floor. By this he will take total eight steps.
But if the receiver come to the 2nd floor and the delivery guy also goes to the 2nd floor, both will have to like cover 4 steps each. 
This will reduce the effort. 


Where two pointers gets applied?
1. This two pointer technique is majorly apllied with Arrays or Linked List Data Structure.
2. It can be applied if the data is in sorted manner, or can be sorted. 
3. It can be used if we have we come across terms like like MERGE, REMOVE, FIND DUPLICATES, RE-ARRANGE values, etc. 
4. It can also be applied where we have to DETECT ANY CYCLE in the LINKED LISTS.
